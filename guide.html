Module 01 · Foundations

GS1 Keys & Barcode Types Explained

Supply chain teams often confuse product barcodes with pallet barcodes. In the GS1 system, each key identifies a different real-world entity — and the SSCC is the unique "license plate" of a physical logistics unit (pallet, carton, roll cage, or parcel).

SSCC · AI (00) — Logistics Unit18 digits

Uniquely identifies one specific physical pallet or shipment unit. Never repeated on another pallet (like a vehicle license plate). Used in EDI DESADV and warehouse receiving gates.

GTIN · AI (01) / (02) — Trade Item8 / 12 / 13 / 14

Identifies a product SKU (e.g. "Box of 12 Olive Oil Bottles"). Every identical box carries the same GTIN. On pallet labels, AI (02) tells the WMS what GTIN is packed inside the pallet.

GLN · AI (410) / (414) — Location13 digits

Identifies a company, warehouse, or loading dock (e.g. Sonae Azambuja Hub). Used in EDI NAD+CZ / NAD+SU headers to route shipments accurately.

Attribute AIs · (10) (15) (37) (400)Variable

Dynamic shipment metadata attached to a pallet: (10) Batch/Lot, (15) Best Before Date YYMMDD, (37) Trade Item Count, and (400) Customer Purchase Order.

Anatomy of an 18-Digit SSCC

Every SSCC has strictly 18 digits (preceded visually by Application Identifier (00), which is not counted in the 18 digits). Try editing the Extension Digit, GS1 Company Prefix, or Serial below to see how the structure and Modulo 10 check digit adapt live:

Full SSCC-18: 356012345000000012 · Issuing GS1 MO: 🇵🇹 Portugal (560) Open in Label Studio →
  • AI (00): Signals to GS1-128 scanners and WMS parsers that the next 18 digits are an SSCC.
  • Extension Digit (0–9): Chosen by your company to increase numbering capacity or classify packaging level (e.g. 0 = Euro-pallet, 3 = mixed pallet).
  • GS1 Company Prefix (GCP): Officially licensed by your local GS1 Member Organisation (7–10 digits). The first 3 digits identify the issuing GS1 country (e.g. 560 Portugal, 840–849 Spain, 400–440 Germany).
  • Serial Reference: Assigned sequentially by your ERP/WMS so no two pallets ever collide. Its length adjusts automatically so 1 + GCP + Serial = 17 digits.
  • Check Digit (18th digit): Computed with Modulo 10 to immediately catch scan errors or manual typos.
Important: The GS1 prefix identifies the organisation that issued the company prefix — not the country of manufacture or origin of the goods.

How a Compliant GS1 Pallet Label Works

A GS1 Logistics Label (typically A5 · 148×210 mm in European retail, or 4×6" · 100×150 mm on Zebra thermal printers) is always organized into three standardized horizontal zones from top to bottom:

Zone 1 (Top) — Company & Routing Info

Human-readable header: Consignor (FROM), Consignee (TO), Purchase Order reference, gross weight, and pallet count (e.g. Pallet 1 of 24).

Zone 2 (Middle) — Trade Details & Secondary Multi-AI

Required by most European supermarket chains for homogeneous pallets. Combines (02) GTIN + (15) Expiry + (37) Quantity + (10) Lot in one GS1-128 barcode, plus optional Sunrise 2027 2D QR Digital Link.

Zone 3 (Bottom) — Mandatory SSCC-18 Barcode

Always at the very bottom of the label so forklift scanners read it cleanly. Minimum bar height: 32 mm. Mandatory white Quiet Zones ≥ 10X on both left and right edges.

Physical Placement Rules on the Pallet (GS1 Standard)

  • Two Adjacent Faces: Apply 2 identical labels on adjacent sides (one short side + one long side) — or all 4 sides for Amazon LTL pallets.
  • Height from Floor: Place between 400 mm and 800 mm from the base of the pallet so automated conveyor scanners never miss the barcode.
  • Edge Clearance: Keep at least 50 mm away from vertical pallet edges (never wrap a barcode around a corner!).
  • Stretch-Wrap Contrast: Apply over or cleanly beneath clear, non-wrinkled stretch film; wrinkled film causes laser refraction errors.
Module 04 · Math & Implementation

Check Digit Algorithm (GS1 Modulo 10) & Code

Given the 17-digit SSCC body (without check digit and without AI 00):

  1. Starting from the rightmost digit, multiply alternating digits by 3 and 1.
  2. Sum all 17 products together.
  3. Check digit = (10 - (sum mod 10)) mod 10.

Worked Example · Body: 35601234500000001

Pos (R→L) 123456789 1011121314151617
Digit 100000005 43210653
Weight 313131313 13131313
Product 3000000015 492301859
Sum = 68 · Check digit = (10 − (68 mod 10)) mod 10 = (10 − 8) mod 10 = 2 → Full SSCC: 356012345000000012
Fast validation trick: Including the check digit (with weight 1 at the rightmost position), any valid SSCC satisfies totalSum % 10 === 0. Try any GS1 key (GTIN-8/12/13/14, GLN, SSCC) in our Interactive Calculator. 
/** Calculate GS1 Modulo 10 check digit for a 17-digit SSCC body. */
function getCheckDigit(body17) {
    let sum = 0;
    const digits = body17.split('').reverse();
    for (let i = 0; i < digits.length; i++) {
        sum += parseInt(digits[i], 10) * (i % 2 === 0 ? 3 : 1);
    }
    return (10 - (sum % 10)) % 10;
}

/** Normalise & validate 17, 18, or 20-digit (AI 00) SSCC strings. */
function validateSSCC(raw) {
    let d = String(raw).replace(/\D/g, '');
    if (d.length === 20 && d.startsWith('00')) d = d.slice(2);
    if (d.length !== 18) return { valid: false, error: 'Expected 18 digits' };
    const expected = getCheckDigit(d.slice(0, 17));
    const provided = parseInt(d[17], 10);
    return { valid: provided === expected, sscc: d, expected, provided };
}

validateSSCC('(00)356012345000000012'); // { valid: true, expected: 2, provided: 2 }
def calculate_sscc_check_digit(body17: str) -> int:
    """Calculates the GS1 Modulo 10 check digit for a 17-digit SSCC payload."""
    if len(body17) != 17 or not body17.isdigit():
        raise ValueError("SSCC payload must be exactly 17 digits")
    digits = [int(d) for d in reversed(body17)]
    total = sum(d * (3 if i % 2 == 0 else 1) for i, d in enumerate(digits))
    return (10 - (total % 10)) % 10

def validate_sscc(raw: str) -> bool:
    """Validates 18-digit or 20-digit (AI 00) SSCC strings."""
    clean = "".join(ch for ch in raw if ch.isdigit())
    if len(clean) == 20 and clean.startswith("00"):
        clean = clean[2:]
    return len(clean) == 18 and int(clean[-1]) == calculate_sscc_check_digit(clean[:17])

print(validate_sscc("(00)356012345000000012"))  # True

4 Classic Mistakes That Break Warehouse Receiving

  • Keeping the leading 00 AI: Laser scanners output 20 digits (00 + 18-digit SSCC). Always strip 00 before storing in SAP EXIDV or computing the check digit.
  • Excel scientific notation corruption: Opening an SSCC CSV in Excel turns 356012345000000012 into 3.56012E+17 and zeroes out the final digits! Always store SSCCs as text strings (never float/int64 in spreadsheets).
  • Forgetting FNC1 (>8) in Zebra ZPL: Without >8, Zebra prints plain Code 128 instead of GS1-128 — retail dock scanners will reject the pallet.
  • Reusing serial numbers too early: GS1 rules prohibit reusing an SSCC within 12 months (best practice: never reuse).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my scanner read a 20-digit SSCC?

GS1-128 barcode scanners include the 2-digit Application Identifier 00 ahead of the 18-digit SSCC. Always strip the leading 00 before validating or storing the code.

Can the GS1 Company Prefix (GCP) length vary?

Yes. Depending on the allocation assigned by your GS1 Member Organization, the GCP is between 7 and 10 digits. The Serial Reference length adjusts automatically so the total SSCC is always 18 digits.

Is the SSCC check digit algorithm identical to GTIN-13 / GTIN-14?

Yes — every GS1 numeric key uses the exact same Modulo 10 (alternating weights 3 and 1 from right to left).

Can I reuse an SSCC number?

GS1 General Specifications forbid reusing an SSCC for at least 12 months after shipment. Best practice for global traceability is never to reuse serial references.
