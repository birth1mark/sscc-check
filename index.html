Quick Guide & Supported Formats
  • Instant Validation (18 or 20 digits): Paste full SSCCs (with or without AI 00) to verify the Modulo 10 check digit and identify country prefix.
  • Check Digit Generation (17 digits): Paste a 17-digit payload to automatically compute and append the 18th GS1 check digit.
  • Range Expansion: Enter START - END (up to 500 codes) to expand and compute check digits for an entire sequence.
  • Full Pallet Labels vs. Barcode Only: Generate complete GS1 A5/A6/4×6" pallet labels or switch to Barcode Only (SSCC Strip) to export standalone .svg, 300 DPI .png, or .zpl barcodes to compose in your own software.
  • EDI & ERP File Extraction: Drop any EDIFACT (GIN+BJ, RFF+SI), SAP IDoc (EXIDV), XML, or CSV file to extract all SSCCs automatically.
  • Hardware Laser Scanners: USB and Bluetooth HID barcode scanners are detected automatically anywhere on the page.
Frequently Asked Questions

Can I generate commercial SSCCs without being registered with GS1?
Valid, globally unique SSCCs legally require an official GS1 Company Prefix (GCP) issued to your organization by a GS1 Member Organization (e.g. GS1 Portugal, GS1 US, GS1 UK).

What formats are returned for labels and barcodes?
In the Web Studio you can export Print / PDF (A5, A6, 4×6" or standalone barcode strips), Zebra ZPL-II (.zpl), Pure Vector (.svg), and High-Res 300 DPI Raster (.png). Via the REST API (POST /v1/labels, GET /v1/barcode, GET /v1/qr) we return application/json, text/plain (ZPL-II), text/html, and image/svg+xml.

Need a custom retailer label template or bespoke ERP feature?
Click Request Custom Features to contact us for custom ZPL templates (Sonae, Jerónimo Martins, Mercadona, Amazon), SAP/Odoo connectors, or dedicated features.
